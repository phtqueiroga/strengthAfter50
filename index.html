Objection handling

Answer the questions people ask before they ever enquire.

The page should resolve fear, confusion, and comparison anxiety before it asks for commitment.

I’ve never touched a barbell. Is this still for me?

Yes. The programme is designed as a beginner on-ramp, with small-group coaching and progression built for first-timers over 50.

I’m active already. Why not just keep walking or doing classes?

Because activity is not the same as structured strength. This programme adds the missing pillar that helps protect muscle, function, and confidence later in life.

What if I’m worried about injury or gym intimidation?

That concern is common, which is why the offer relies on coached technique, small groups, and evidence-based programming instead of a general free-for-all.

Why include a DEXA scan?

It turns vague wellness into a measurable baseline and helps people see visible progress over the first eight weeks.
